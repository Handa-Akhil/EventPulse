# EventPulse Chatbot FAQ

## 1. What is EventPulse?
EventPulse is a full-stack event discovery and booking app. Users can log in, discover nearby events, get recommendations, book tickets, view e-tickets, receive notifications, and submit events for approval.

## 2. How do I sign up?
Go to the auth page, switch to Signup, enter your full name, email, password, and confirm password, then create the account.

## 3. What happens after signup?
After signup, EventPulse opens a preference onboarding modal so the dashboard can be personalized.

## 4. What preferences can I choose?
The onboarding preferences are Movies, Comedy, Sports, Concerts, Theatre, and Festivals.

## 5. How do I log in?
Use your registered email and password on the login form.

## 6. Does EventPulse support Google login?
Yes. Google login works when the Google account email matches an existing EventPulse account.

## 7. What if Google login says no account was found?
That means the Google email does not match an existing EventPulse account. Sign up first or use the same email as your existing account.

## 8. How does forgot password work?
Enter your registered email, request an OTP, verify the OTP, and set a new password.

## 9. How long is the password reset OTP valid?
The OTP expires in 10 minutes.

## 10. What can I do on the dashboard?
You can see recommendations, browse nearby events, search events, filter by category, switch between grid, calendar, and map views, change location, open My Tickets, and create an event.

## 11. How are events personalized?
Recommendations are based on your saved preferences and your selected or saved location.

## 12. What range does EventPulse use for discovery?
Event discovery is filtered within 40 km of the selected or detected location.

## 13. Can I change my city manually?
Yes. The dashboard includes a city dropdown so you can switch locations manually.

## 14. Can I use my current device location?
Yes. Use the current-location button and allow browser location access.

## 15. What if location access is denied?
EventPulse falls back to a saved or default city.

## 16. How do I find nearby events?
Use the dashboard search bar, category chips, and view modes. Nearby events appear based on location and filters.

## 17. What event views are available?
The dashboard supports grid view, calendar view, and map view.

## 18. What can I see on an event details page?
You can see the title, category, venue, date, description, highlights, showtimes, price, availability, and other event details.

## 19. How do I book an event?
Open an event, choose a showtime, choose ticket quantity, and confirm the booking from the event details page.

## 20. Can I book a sold-out event?
No. Sold-out events cannot be booked.

## 21. What happens after booking?
You get a booking confirmation and can open your e-ticket with a QR code.

## 22. Where can I see my bookings?
Open the My Tickets page from the dashboard top bar.

## 23. What does My Tickets show?
It shows your booked events, date, time slot, quantity, total amount, reference code, and e-ticket access.

## 24. Does EventPulse support e-tickets?
Yes. Successful bookings can open an e-ticket with a QR code.

## 25. Can I review an event?
Yes. Signed-in users can submit a star rating and an optional comment.

## 26. Can I review the same event twice?
No. The UI prevents the same signed-in user from reviewing the same event twice.

## 27. How do notifications work?
The notification bell shows your recent notifications, unread count, and supports mark-as-read actions.

## 28. Are notifications real time?
Yes. EventPulse supports real-time notification updates through sockets.

## 29. Can I mark all notifications as read?
Yes. The notification dropdown includes a mark all read action.

## 30. How do I create an event?
Open Create Event, fill in the event form, and submit it for review.

## 31. What information is required to create an event?
The current form collects title, category, city, venue, price, total seats, event date, and a short description.

## 32. What categories are available in the Create Event form?
The current creation categories are Music, Tech, Art, Sports, and Business.

## 33. Is a created event published immediately?
No. User-created events are submitted for admin approval first.

## 34. Does EventPulse have an admin panel?
Yes. EventPulse includes a separate admin login and admin panel.

## 35. What should the chatbot say for unrelated questions?
Sorry, I cant help with that. I can only answer questions related to EventPulse.
